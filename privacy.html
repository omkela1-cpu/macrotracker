Privacy policy

20 October 2026

MacroTracker is a small personal app run by Om Kela (contact below). It has no adverts and no tracking, and your information is never sold.

What's stored and why

Your email, password and what you log (food, weigh-ins, goals and the details you enter) are saved to your account so the app works and your diary isn't lost. They're stored with Supabase, the database service the app uses. Only you can see your diary.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on, the app saves your food and weigh-ins to Health, and reads your weight and steps from it. Weigh-ins it reads are saved to your account; steps stay on your phone. Health data is only used for the app's features, never for advertising.

Other services, only when you use them

  • Open Food Facts: barcodes and search words are sent to look up foods. If you choose to share a product, its details and photos become public there, without your name.
  • Claude: if you connect it, Claude can read and add to your diary when you ask.

Your choices

Your information is kept until you delete your account (You > Account > Delete account), which removes everything saved to it. You can also ask for a copy, a correction or deletion by email.
